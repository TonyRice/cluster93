# Infrastructure Dedication

Cluster93 (`c93`) is dedicated as sovereign, open-source compute infrastructure for
the **Asgardian Space Nation** and its digital ecosystem.

## Purpose & Rationale

Modern digital infrastructure is heavily centralized within a handful of terrestrial
cloud providers. True sovereignty — whether for independent communities, distributed
collectives, or spacefaring nations — requires computational self-sufficiency:

- **Zero Cloud Lock-in:** Stand up clustered, sandboxed, distributed execution fabrics
  on bare metal, self-hosted clusters, or edge environments.
- **Resilient Distributed State:** Dynamic peer discovery and an in-memory data grid
  (Apache Ignite) that heals automatically across network partitions.
- **Autonomous Operability:** A native Model Context Protocol (MCP) server enabling AI
  agents to manage, scale, and dispatch workloads without a human bottleneck.

## Heritage & Homage

- **Messier 93 (NGC 2447):** An open star cluster in Puppis, the stern of the
  constellation *Argo Navis* — a self-contained, luminous cluster navigating from the
  helm of human exploration. Named in homage by Charles Messier's catalogue (1781).
- **Lineage:** Cluster93 is the modern continuation of **Automately**, a clustered,
  distributed backend platform first published in 2014 and acquired before 2018.
  Cluster93 is a branch of that pre-acquisition codebase. The original open-source
  organization lived at `github.com/Automately` (`automate.ly`); its archived home is
  preserved at
  <https://web.archive.org/web/20160204012506/https://github.com/Automately>.

  > *In memoriam — Automately. Founded 2014; open-sourced and acquired before 2018.
  > The name is retired; the lineage carries on.*

- **Personal Heritage:** A nod to 1993.
